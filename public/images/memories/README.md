# Yatra Memories & Photo Gallery Folder

Place all your **Pilgrim Memories, Guest Highlights, Group Photos, and Candid Moments** here.

### 📸 What to place in this folder:
- **Yatri group photos** at Jolingkong, Gunji, and Nabhidhang
- **Homestay cultural evenings** with Rung community hosts in Nabi / Napalchu
- **Aarti by the sacred fire** and devotional darshan moments
- **Trekking snapshots** at Parvati Sarovar & Gauri Kund
- **Alpine sunrise & sunset captures** along the Kumaon route

### 🔗 How to reference your image in code:
```ts
// In src/components/GallerySection.tsx (DESTINATION_GALLERY):
image: "/images/memories/your-memory-photo.jpg"
```

### 💡 Recommended Dimensions:
- **Size**: 1200 x 800 px or 1080 x 1080 px
- **Format**: `.jpg`, `.jpeg`, `.webp`, `.png`
