# Destination Images Folder

Place all your **Destination and Expedition Photos** here.

### 📍 What to place in this folder:
- **Adi Kailash** peak and high-altitude views
- **Om Parvat** and Nabhidhang viewpoint photos
- **Parvati Sarovar & Gauri Kund** alpine lake views
- **Gunji, Nabi, Kuti & Napalchu** village and homestay landscapes
- **Lipulekh Pass, Kalapani, Sheshnag Parvat** border landscapes
- **Kumaon & Garhwal** scenic routes and mountain passes

### 🔗 How to reference your image in code:
```ts
// In src/data/destinationsData.ts or src/data/mockData.ts:
image: "/images/destinations/your-file-name.jpg"
```

### 💡 Recommended Dimensions:
- **Size**: 1200 x 800 px or 1920 x 1080 px (16:9 or 4:3)
- **Format**: `.jpg`, `.jpeg`, `.webp`, `.png`
